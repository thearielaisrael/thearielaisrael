## 🌸 About Me

I’m an experienced software developer and Health Services Management student intentionally transitioning into **health information, healthcare data, and informatics**.

My technical background includes structured data, SQL and relational databases, digital systems, documentation, quality assurance, accessibility, troubleshooting, and operational workflows. Most recently, I served as the sole full-stack developer for Walton Arts Center and Walmart AMP, building and maintaining production applications used by staff and the public.

I’m now building the healthcare-specific experience that will connect this technical foundation to health information management and informatics.

My immediate focus is contributing in roles involving:

- 🗂 Health information and medical records
- 📄 Release of information and documentation workflows
- 📊 Healthcare data and information quality
- 🏥 Healthcare operations and administrative support
- 💻 Health technology and systems support

Long term, I’m building toward **Women’s Health Equity Advocacy & Informatics**, connecting health information, research, data, and patient-centered technology to support clearer information and more equitable care.

> The advocacy and research fuel the technology, not the other way around.
